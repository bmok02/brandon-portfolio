# Surface: home + case studies (clean & classic)

Scope: `/` and `/work/[slug]`. Mode: Experience (portfolio).
Audience: recruiters, PMs, hiring managers (30-second scan, depth on demand).
User path: kopitiam table (too messy) → design canvas (too structural / AI slop) → Flatirons trail (not professional enough) → chose the standing exit "Clean & classic", benchmark "designer-PM portfolios" (Brian Lovin, Rauno Freiberg): minimal personal sites, warm details, small delightful interactions. Content from INTERVIEW-NOTES.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet, precise personal site where the writing and the work carry everything; the only flourish is the set of small hand-drawn objects, one per story. Refuses the hero-banner + card-grid template and every themed world tried before.

OWN-WORLD: Neutral warm-grey paper (light) and soft charcoal (dark), one ink colour for text, one accent (a deep blue) for links and focus only. One sans family, a tight type scale, hairline rules, no cards, no shadows on chrome. The hand-drawn objects are the only illustration and the only colour.

STORY: Visitor reads who Brandon is in three sentences, scans a grouped list of work (Building / Working / Community), opens any item for a short case study, sees where he has learned, and emails him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Narrow left-aligned column. Name, role line, a three-sentence intro in his voice, small facts and links. Immediately below, the start of "Selected work": rows with a small object, title, one line, year.

FORM: Canon (standing exit) on seed 4ffd9020, benchmarked to designer-PM portfolios at their craft level.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
