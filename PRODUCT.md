## Product Principles

1. People first: every story names who it was for and who it was made with.
2. Show the learning, not just the win.
3. Respect the 30-second reader: the plain answer is always one click away.
4. Play rewards curiosity but never blocks content.
5. Tell the truth: high-level where confidential, placeholders where facts are missing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Every work row is a real link with a visible title; one linear reading order; full keyboard navigation; prefers-reduced-motion stops the hover motion and steam. Light and dark follow the system. WCAG AA contrast.
